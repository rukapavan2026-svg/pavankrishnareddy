What is a Carbon Footprint?

A carbon footprint is the total amount of greenhouse gases (primarily carbon dioxide) produced by human activities. It measures the impact of our daily choices on the environment. The average person produces about 4 metric tons of CO₂ per year, but this varies significantly based on lifestyle and location.

How to Reduce Your Carbon Footprint

🚴 Transportation

  • Use public transit or carpool
  • Consider electric vehicles
  • Walk or bike for short trips
  • Reduce flying when possible

⚡ Home Energy

  • Switch to renewable energy
  • Improve insulation
  • Use LED light bulbs
  • Install a programmable thermostat

🥗 Food & Diet

  • Reduce meat consumption
  • Buy local and seasonal
  • Reduce food waste
  • Compost organic waste

♻️ Consumption

  • Buy less, buy quality
  • Choose sustainable brands
  • Recycle and reuse
  • Thrift and secondhand

💡 General Tips

  • Offset carbon emissions
  • Support green initiatives
  • Track your progress
  • Educate others

🌱 Long-term Changes

  • Solar panel installation
  • Electric vehicle purchase
  • Plant trees
  • Support carbon removal
